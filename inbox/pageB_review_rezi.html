Quick answer
4.2/ 5 · score generated from parse data, not editor opinion

Rezi is worth it if your #1 worry is machine readability. Its default templates parsed 9 of 10 tracked fields in Greenhouse and 8 of 10 in Lever — among the best results in our current batch. The trade-off: its AI-written bullet points read generic for specialized roles, so budget time to rewrite content yourself. If you mainly need free one-off checks rather than a builder, a paid subscription is overkill — see alternatives below.

Score formula: 70% field-level parse performance · 20% export freedom · 10% pricing transparency. Commissions are not an input.

What we measured

We generated the same fictional candidate's resume using Rezi's three most popular templates, exported to PDF, and uploaded each to live Greenhouse and Lever application forms — the same protocol as our ATS Parse Study. No submissions were made; we recorded the autofill parse of 10 fields per run.

Who should use Rezi

Get it if you…

  • Apply mostly to mid/large companies that run structured ATS pipelines
  • Want keyword targeting against a specific job description built into the editor
  • Prefer a one-time payment — the lifetime deal beats 3+ months of subscription

Skip it if you…

  • Work in a niche field — its AI bullets read generic and you'll rewrite most of them
  • Only need to check an existing resume, not build one (use a free parse check instead)
  • Apply mainly to small companies that read PDFs by hand — parse optimization buys you little there

FAQ

Does Rezi guarantee my resume passes ATS screening?

No tool can. Parsing affects whether your data is extracted and searchable — it's one variable. Content quality, role fit and competition matter more. Rezi's parse performance was strong in our tests; that's the claim we can verify, and the only one we'll make.

Is the Rezi score based on your opinion?

The 4.2 is computed from field-level parse records (70%), export freedom (20%) and pricing transparency (10%). Editors write the words; the number comes from the data. Commissions are not an input — Teal, which pays us nothing, outranks two paying tools in this category.

When was this review last tested?

June 2026, batch GH-2026Q2-rezi. We re-test quarterly; if Rezi ships a template update that changes results, the score changes with it and the changelog will say so.
